Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Habitude 1.0.0

Short version

Habitude is designed as a local-first habit and activity tracker. Daily activity statuses, notes, activities, and preferences are stored in your browser. Normal local tracking does not require an application server.

Data stored on your device

Habitude stores activities, daily statuses, notes, preferences, and synchronization metadata in browser local storage. This data remains in the browser profile unless you export it, synchronize it, or otherwise choose to share it. Clearing browser site data or resetting the app can remove local data.

Daily notes and activity records

Your activity names, daily status choices, notes, and report data are processed in the browser to provide tracking and analysis. Habitude does not upload these records to a Habitude-operated application server as part of normal local use.

Backups and exports

When you export a full JSON backup or CSV file, the file is generated for you to save to a location you choose. You are responsible for protecting exported files, because they may contain personal routines, statuses, and notes. Importing or restoring a backup changes local data as described in the app.

Google Drive synchronization

Google Drive synchronization is optional and is initiated by you. When you connect and use it, Habitude requests authorization through Google's identity services and reads or writes the app's JSON synchronization file in your Google Drive using the requested Drive file scope. The synchronized file may contain activities, settings, daily statuses, notes, and synchronization metadata. Google's own privacy terms and policies also apply.

Third-party services and browser APIs

Google identity and Drive services are third-party services used only for the optional Drive feature. Their availability and handling of information are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Browser features such as file downloads, sharing, installation, and offline storage vary by device and browser.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Habitude does not require an application analytics or advertising system for its normal tracking workflow. It does not require a Habitude-operated account. Third-party hosting providers and optional Google services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Offline and PWA behavior

When installed and when the service worker has been registered by the hosted app, Habitude can cache its app shell and supporting files for offline use. Google Drive synchronization requires an internet connection. Local tracking and analysis are designed to remain available without connecting to Google Drive.

Your control

You choose what to record, what to export, and whether to connect Google Drive. You can export a backup before clearing browser data or resetting the app. Disconnecting Google Drive stops future sync actions from that session, but does not delete a synchronization file already stored in your Drive.

Questions

Questions about this deployment's privacy practices can be raised through the project's published repository or contact information.
